# Agon Rulebooks v1.0: what changed from Agon *V1* (Homebrewery)

## The split
- **World Lore** (Book I): The Great Game, Sword Fall, the Current Age, the city-states, No Man's Land, factions, the Frenzied, commerce, communication, the Aegis and the binding of familiars. Also lore copied from the rules: death and resurrection, the four Theion colors, and a pointer to the gods table.
- **Player's Handbook** (Book II): Core Rules, Character Creation, Divine Power, Equipment & Economy, and the appendices (Gods & Affinities, Spell List, Boons and Curses, Items).
- **Historian's Handbook** (Book III): a new short intro, "Rules You Set" (a checklist that links to the Historian-choice rules), Rewards, the full **Didra Market** (moved out of Equipment; the Player's Handbook keeps a short pointer), the "For the Historian" notes, and the full **Bestiary**.
- Nothing was dropped. A word-level comparison against the original shows only the edits below.

## Fixes (light edit)
- **Hecate** in the gods table: *Shadow* was in the Conceptual column. Now Primordial: Fire, Ice, Shadow; Conceptual: Deceit, Duality, Animality (matches the campaign lore).
- **Wrath** card listed *Hera, Athena*; the gods table says **Zeus, Hera, Poseidon**. Card now matches the table.
- **Violence** card listed *Kratos, Nike*; the table says **Ares, Kratos**. Card now matches the table.
- **Sovereign of Death** renamed **Sovereign of the Dead** to match the gods table.
- **The four fundamental spells:** Part IV said "three spells are the most basic" and put Spinthēr in the appendix, while the appendix and Part III said four. Spinthēr now has its card with Belos, Aspis, and Potamos in *The Fundamental Spells*; the Spell List points there.
- **Speed** box said "in feet"; now meters (the rule was already 9 m).
- **Agathós Daemons:** "any of the three Action rolls" (old Big 3 wording) is now "any roll".
- Every "pg ##", "Part V", "Appendix 2/3", and "Attachment ##" reference is now a working link, including links between books.
- The character sheet note now points to the interactive sheet; the Roll20 mention now points to the sheet's Theion gauge and Owlbear.
- Typos: deduct → deduce, induct → draw, carapice, intagible, weilded, modifer (×2), airborn, Apthrodite, Dionysis, elemensts, halfed, Primoridal, diety (×2), Goeteia, harrasser, "my filling", "mileage will very", "totalling", "an non-player", "its their turn", "demigod", "damcoles"; missing periods; Homebrewery `---` dashes; prices like `80'o` → `80o`.

## Rulings (Nov 5, after CJ's go-ahead)
- **Tenacity:** the save is now an **Endurance Trial, TN 16** (Close Call counts). With the +1 Endurance from Tenacity alone that's 55%, 65% at +4, and 85% at the +8 cap. It's a real tank feature, but not a guarantee, once per Full Rest.
- **Charm (inspire):** now a **Wit Trial, TN 12** (Standard). It's a Daily use for a +1d4, so it should usually land.
- **Monster traits:** the 4/6 trait caps are for characters only. Monsters may go to 5–6 to show inhuman scale (Ancients, Legendaries, Mythics). The rule is written into Monster Rules. No stat blocks changed.
- **Player count:** "minimum 3–4 recommended" stays. A party of 3–4 pairs is what Mythic encounters are built for.

## Art (v1.2, Nov 5)
Every borrowed placeholder image is gone. The books now use two kinds of art, both free to use:
- **Public-domain prints** (CC0, The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access), shown with an ink-on-parchment treatment that inverts to pale lines on black in dark mode:
  - *The Fall*: Giuseppe Piattoli, *The Sword of Damocles* (1785–1807), pen and ink. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/340475
  - *The Current Age*: Giovanni Battista Piranesi, ruins of the Temple of Concord, *Vedute di Roma* (c. 1750–78). https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/406668
- **Original black-figure vignettes** drawn for Agon in a Greek Geometric style (tools/vignettes.py → site/art/): No Man's Land, the Exchange, the Covenant, Resurrection, the Game (Big 3), Renown (Reputation), Divine Power.
