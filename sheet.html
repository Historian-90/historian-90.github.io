Speed

Speed = 9 m + 1 per Agility point, minus any Speed loss. Holding a Concentration spell halves it.

Evasion

Evasion = 10 + Agility points − armor penalty. An attack roll must meet or beat it to hit you. Dodge and Parry raise it against one attack.

AGON · 1.0

Attack

After an attack roll against you, spend a Reaction to raise your Evasion against that attack. You get one per round (two while wielding your Aegis, never both on the same attack). A Divine Strike can't be dodged or parried.

Riposte: if your Parry turned the hit into a Miss, strike back right away (part of the same Reaction).

The Covenant Holds: if a hit dropped you to 0 HP or below while you wield your Aegis, it can take the rest onto its own HP, leaving you at 1.
